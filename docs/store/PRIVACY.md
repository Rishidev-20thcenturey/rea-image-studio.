# Privacy policy — REA Image Studio

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

REA Image Studio is an image editor that runs entirely on your device. This policy says what the
app does with your information, in full. It is short because the app does almost nothing with it.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** There is no account, no sign-in, and no server belonging to us. The app has no internet
permission at all, so it cannot send your data anywhere even in principle.

## Where your images go

Images you open, and documents you save, stay where you put them: in the app's private storage while
a document is open, and in the folder you choose — normally `Downloads/REA Image Studio` — when you
export or save. We never receive a copy. Files you import are read from the location you pick and
written back only where you ask.

## Diagnostics

The app keeps a local log of its own startup and errors, so a crash can be understood. It is stored
in the app's private folder on your device, it is never uploaded, and you can read, copy or clear it
from **About ▸ Diagnostics**. If you choose to copy it and send it to us yourself, that is your
decision and it is the only way any log ever leaves your device.

## Purchases

The app is sold as a paid download through Google Play. The payment and the record of your purchase
are handled by Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never
see your card, your address, or your Google account details. We receive only the aggregate sales
figures Google reports to every developer.

## Third-party code

The editor's engine is open-source software (PhotoCraft and PhotoDroid, MIT and Apache-2.0) and it
uses further open-source libraries. None of them contains advertising, analytics or tracking code,
and the full list, with licences, is in the app under **About ▸ Licences**.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. Uninstalling
the app removes its private storage; files you exported remain in your own folders until you delete
them. If you believe we hold something about you, write to the address below.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version ships in the
next release of the app.
